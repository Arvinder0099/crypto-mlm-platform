⚡ REAL-TIME EARNING SYSTEM - HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Timeline:
─────────

11:00 AM - Cron Job Starts
    ↓
11:00 AM - Find all active investments
    ↓
11:00 AM - Calculate daily earning for each:
    Formula: (investment_amount × plan_dailyEarn%) / 100
    Example: (2000 × 10%) / 100 = 200 USDT
    ↓
11:00 AM - Credit earnings to wallet
    ↓
11:00 AM - Create transaction record
    ↓
11:00 AM - Update investment earning history
    ↓
11:00 AM - Check if investment complete (duration expired)
    ├─ YES → Change status to 'completed', add total return
    └─ NO → Continue as active
    ↓
11:00 AM - Update dashboard (real-time)
    ↓
User sees updated balance & earning history instantly!

Frequency: EVERY HOUR (on the hour)
Location: backend/server-complete.js line ~873
